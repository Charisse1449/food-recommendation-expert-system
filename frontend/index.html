About the System

A rule-based food recommendation expert system.

Prolog Knowledge Base

Each dish is represented by a category and the ingredients it requires. The dashboard totals are calculated from those facts.

Recommendation Rules

The system ranks dishes by the percentage of required ingredients you have and lists any ingredients still missing.

Favorites and History

Your saved recipes and recommendation history stay in this browser. They are not sent to an account or external service.

Local Web Application

The page is served by SWI-Prolog on your computer and uses the existing Prolog recommendation logic.
